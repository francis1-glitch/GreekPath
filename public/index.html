The Problem

Managing compliance across dozens
of chapters shouldn't be this hard.

Greek life administrators are drowning in manual processes. Here's what that looks like day-to-day.

📊
Disconnected spreadsheets and PDFs
Tracking compliance across 60+ chapters means juggling dozens of files — different formats, different owners, no single source of truth.
🕐
Scorecard data that's a semester old
By the time you compile and distribute compliance reports, the data is stale. Problems that could have been caught in October don't surface until March.
📧
Incident histories buried in email
When something happens, reconstructing the full history of a chapter's incidents means digging through years of inboxes, forwarded threads, and attached PDFs.
🏛
No visibility for national headquarters
National HQs have no easy way to monitor their chapters across campuses. Each university uses its own system — or no system at all.
Platform Features

Everything your compliance
office actually needs.

Built from the ground up for Greek life — not retrofitted from generic compliance software.

Core Compliance
🟢
Real-Time Compliance Dashboard
Green, yellow, and red status for every chapter — updated live. See your entire portfolio at a glance, with drill-down into any chapter.
✅
Automated Compliance Checklists
Configurable checklists with deadline tracking, automated reminders, and escalation alerts when chapters fall behind.
📁
Document Submission & Verification
Track insurance certificates, training completions, and new member education plans. Flag missing or expired documents automatically.
🎓
GPA Monitoring & Threshold Alerts
Automatic flagging when a chapter's GPA drops below your configured threshold. Pull in registrar data each term without manual entry.
Roster & Data Management
📤
Bulk Roster Import
Upload rosters via XLSX, CSV, or PDF. Chapters can submit updates directly, and officers can review diffs before approving changes.
🔍
Enrollment Validation
Automatic cross-validation against university enrollment records. Surface discrepancies — suspended students, non-enrolled members — before they become issues.
📋
Member History & Audit Trail
Full lifecycle tracking for every member: join date, officer roles held, academic standing changes, and any associated incidents.
🔄
Semester Transitions
Streamlined new member intake and roll-off workflows. Archive graduating seniors, carry forward officer history, and reset checklists in minutes — not weeks.
Incident & Risk Management
🚨
Incident Reporting & Tracking
Log incidents with structured data — type, severity, parties involved, response timeline. All records are searchable and exportable for legal or accreditation review.
📅
Event Risk Assessment
Pre-event risk checklists for social functions. Require security plans, guest list caps, and sober monitor assignments before approving any event.
⚠️
Risk Score History
Each chapter maintains a running risk score derived from incident history, compliance gaps, and academic standing — so patterns are visible before a crisis.
🔒
Probation & Sanctions Management
Manage active sanctions with required remediation steps, check-in schedules, and automatic status updates when conditions are met or missed.
Reporting & National Headquarters
📊
One-Click Compliance Reports
Generate semester compliance summaries, chapter scorecards, and incident reports as polished PDFs or structured CSV exports — ready for board meetings or accreditors.
🏛
National HQ Portal
Give national headquarters read-only access to their chapters across every campus — without requiring them to log in to each university's system separately.
🔔
Configurable Alerts & Digests
Weekly digest emails, real-time push alerts for critical incidents, and scheduled reminders for upcoming deadlines — all configurable per user role.
🔐
Role-Based Access Control
Separate permission tiers for university admins, IFC/Panhellenic officers, chapter officers, and national HQ — each seeing exactly what they need.
